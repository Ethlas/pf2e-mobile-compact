Your PF2e mobile companion for using your phone at the table. All actions, spells, skills and more you need to play the game, even allowing you to target on the canvas.. Every roll still goes through the PF2e system, so modifiers, MAP and degrees of success stay exactly as the system calculates them.

Why use it

  • All your action from mobile: each weapon shows its attack at every MAP step, plus damage and crit buttons.
  • Target from the header: pick a target from a list that shows how far away each token is, or pick several at once. Flanking is checked for you.
  • Every spellcasting entry on one tab: focus points, slots per rank with + and −, prepared and innate spells, with range, area, save and duration on each spell.
  • Battery saver: optionally skip the map canvas and mute table audio on phones. Targeting, distances and flanking keep working without the map.
  • Chat in the sheet: roll, read the card and apply damage without leaving the sheet or selecting a token.
  • Party and kingdom: a mobile party sheet, and the Kingmaker kingdom sheet when PF2e Kingmaker Tools is active.
  • Physical dice: with the Physical Dice module, roll real dice, type in the result and let PF2e work out the outcome.

Getting started

Enable the module in your world and join from a phone or tablet. The mobile sheet opens on its own, and desktop players keep the normal sheet. The cog menu on the phone allows switches to the desktop view and back. Requires Foundry v14 and PF2e 6.0 or later. Full feature tour on GitHub
