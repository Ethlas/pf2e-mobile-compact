Your PF2e mobile companion for using your phone at the table.
All actions, spells, skills and more you need to play the game, even allowing you to target on the canvas.. Every roll still goes through the PF2e system, so modifiers, MAP and degrees of success stay exactly as the system calculates them.

Features

  • Strikes with an attack button for every MAP step, plus damage and crit
  • All (skill) actions that are on the character sheet
  • Target list with the distance to each token, multiple targets and flanking
  • All spellcasting entries on one tab: focus points, slots per rank, prepared and innate spells
  • Support for staffs with the dailies module enabled
  • Skills, gear and character overview
  • Chat tab; Apply damage from chat cards without selecting a token
  • Party sheet and stash
  • Battery saver: optionally turn off the map canvas and mute audio on phones
  • Keeps the screen awake during the session
  • Recommended: Physical dice entry (with the Physical Dice module)
  • Kingmaker kingdom sheet (with PF2e Kingmaker Tools)

Getting started

Enable the module in your world and join from a phone or tablet. The mobile sheet opens on its own, and desktop players keep the normal sheet. The cog menu on the phone allows switches to the desktop view and back. Requires Foundry v14 and PF2e 6.0 or later. Full feature tour on GitHub
